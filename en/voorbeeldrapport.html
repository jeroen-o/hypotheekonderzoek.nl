    1 Executive summary

    Your position at a glance

    Five key findings

    1. Your market share rose from 6,1% in January to 8,1% in October. That is faster than the peer group (+0,9 percentage points).
    2. You are often included in calculations, but with self-employed and senior clients you lose out on borrowing capacity. In 34% and 29% of those calculations respectively, your maximum mortgage is more than 2% below the market.
    3. Three advisory firms together account for 14% of your applications. Your concentration is higher than in the market.
    4. Your rate for a 10-year fixed period with NHG is below the market median. For 20-year fixed without NHG you are in the most expensive quartile.
    5. Your new business is greener than the market: 53% has label A or better, against 47% in the market. The share of customers with labels E–G who include energy-saving measures in their mortgage lags behind.

    19 Fairness & bias

    Does your underwriting treat groups equally?

    An independent test of whether conversion and acceptance differ between groups, adjusted for income, LTV and customer type. A difference is not proof of discrimination, but a reason to review criteria and models (EBA/GL/2020/06 § 54–55, AI Act).

    Difference in acceptance after adjustment

    Difference in the probability of acceptance versus the reference group, after adjustment for income, LTV, LTI and customer type. Market in brackets.

    Method: logistic regression on aggregated cells of at least 25 applications. "Signal" means a difference of more than 3 percentage points that is statistically significant (p < 0,05). Age and income type are permitted risk factors; the signal does, however, call for a justification.

    Where does the difference lie?

    Contribution of each factor to the difference for self-employed clients (explainability)

    1. Review the income assessment for self-employed clients with less than 3 years of trading history
    2. Document the rationale for the age limit on the loan term for over-60s
    3. Repeat the test every six months and report the outcome to the risk committee

    22 Methodology & competition law

    How the figures are compiled

    Source and definitions

    • Calculations, applications and completions recorded in the mortgage advice software and Blinqx Connect CRM
    • Market share is weighted: your applications divided by the total in the same period
    • Interest rates come from publicly published rate sheets (reference date stated)
    • The energy label is the registered label of the property
    • Figures relate to the Blinqx network, not to the Dutch market as a whole

    Privacy and competition law

    • Aggregated figures only, not traceable to individual consumers or advisers
    • Segments with fewer than 25 observations are not shown
    • Peer groups consist of at least 5 parties, without names
    • No figures for individual competitors and no future or non-public prices
    • Interest rates are shown only after the fact and as a range
