Bidding without a financing condition: does the mortgage complete more often and faster?

An independent impact study of certificate and guarantee products for bidding without a financing condition (financieringsvoorbehoud), and of the role of source validation. What does it deliver for the buyer, seller, estate agent, mortgage adviser and mortgage lender?

1. Background

In a tight housing market, many buyers bid without a financing condition (financieringsvoorbehoud) to make their offer more attractive. That is risky. If the mortgage then falls through, the buyer may owe the penalty set out in the purchase agreement, often 10% of the purchase price.

There are products that limit this risk. The feasibility of the mortgage and the value of the property are assessed in advance, the buyer receives a certificate stating a maximum bid amount, and a guarantee covers the risk if the mortgage is nevertheless declined within the terms. Examples in the Dutch market are Bieden met Zekerheid from BNP Paribas Cardif and BankGarantieZeker from Nationale Waarborg.

What has not yet been measured independently: does a mortgage with such a certificate complete more often and faster, and does the process run more smoothly than without one? We are investigating this using data from mortgage advice.

Hypotheekonderzoek.nl™ is not a provider or distributor of these products. The research is independent, and we do not compare individual providers with one another.

2. How these products work

The products differ by provider, but share a similar structure:

StepWhat happens
1. Upfront assessmentThe mortgage adviser assesses the feasibility and affordability of the mortgage. This is followed by a valuation or an estimate of the property value.
2. CertificateThe buyer receives a certificate stating the maximum amount for which they can responsibly bid. It often applies to a single property and for a limited period.
3. Unconditional bidThe buyer bids without a financing condition, up to the maximum amount on the certificate.
4. Guarantee after purchaseAfter the purchase agreement has been signed and the cooling-off period has passed, the guarantee or bank guarantee is arranged.
5. Cover if declinedIf the mortgage is nevertheless declined within the terms, the provider covers the risk, up to a maximum of 10% of the purchase price or the agreed guarantee amount.

Terms and costs differ by provider and change over time. Please consult the provider for the current terms.

3. Research questions

Main question: does a mortgage with a certificate for bidding without a financing condition complete more often and faster than without one? Does the process run better, with higher conversion, more applications that are first time right and less drop-out? And what does source validation add?

  1. Completion: how often does an accepted bid lead to completion, with and without a certificate?
  2. Conversion: how does the number of calculations relate to applications, binding offers and completions, with and without a certificate?
  3. First time right: how often is the mortgage application accepted at the first attempt, without additional questions, requests for further documents or recalculation?
  4. Lead time: how many working days does each step take, from calculation to completion?
  5. Variance: how large is the difference between the amount on the certificate, the maximum mortgage at the first calculation and the final mortgage?
  6. Who uses it: for which customer types, regions and price brackets are certificates used, and who falls outside the terms, such as self-employed people or flexible workers?
  7. Experience: how do buyers, sellers, estate agents and advisers experience the certainty provided by a certificate?
  8. Source validation: does the process run even better if income and financial obligations have been validated at source in advance using the Procesversneller (process accelerator)? And does it matter how long before the bid this took place, within the 90-day validity period?

4. What we compare

GroupDescription
A. With certificateBid without a financing condition, with a certificate or guarantee product
B. With conditionBid with a financing condition, without a certificate
C. No condition and no certificateBid without a financing condition and without cover: the group with the highest risk for the buyer
A+ and B+The same groups, but with borrowing capacity validated at source in advance using the Procesversneller

We control for customer type, income, LTV, region, price bracket, mortgage lender and period. This way we measure the effect of the product itself, not the difference in the customers who use it.

Fully measurable per case
All groups can be determined directly from the case files in eBlinqx, because each case records:
  • whether a certificate for bidding without a condition was used
  • whether the bid was made with or without a financing condition
  • whether and when source data was retrieved using the Procesversneller
  • which items come from the source: income, pension, financial obligations and property ownership
  • the dates of calculation, application, binding offer and completion
No assumptions or links to external sources are therefore needed to form the groups.

6. Source validation

The upfront assessment is only as reliable as the data it is based on. If income, financial obligations and property ownership are retrieved directly from the source, with the customer's consent, the final mortgage deviates less from the certificate. The aim is 100% source validation.

The Procesversneller in eBlinqx
In the Blinqx advice software, customers can use DigiD (Dutch government login) to retrieve their data from official sources. The adviser loads this validated source data into the mortgage fact-find and the financial plan in a few clicks. The source data remains usable for an application for 90 days. Because this happens in the same software as the calculation, the application and the completion, eBlinqx records per case when the source data was retrieved and which items come from the source, such as income, pension and financial obligations. This allows us to measure both the effect of the Procesversneller and the validation rate per item and per customer type, as well as how many days before the bid or application validation took place. 7. Benefits per party

Buyer

  • Makes a stronger bid, with less risk of the 10% penalty
  • Knows reliably in advance how much they can borrow
  • A faster process through to completion

Seller

  • More certainty that the sale will go through
  • Less risk of the agreement being dissolved
  • A more predictable transfer

Estate agent

  • Can better assess the feasibility of bids
  • More purchase agreements that go through
  • Objective substantiation to present to the seller

Mortgage adviser

  • Insight into which product works best for which customer type
  • Higher conversion and more applications that are first time right
  • Fewer recalculations and less work on the case file

Mortgage lender

  • Insight into the quality of inflow with a certificate
  • Less drop-out and shorter lead times
  • Substantiation for underwriting policy and partnerships

Certificate and guarantee providers

  • An independent, aggregated impact assessment of their product
  • Insight into groups that currently fall outside the terms
  • Insight into reach by region and price bracket

9. Conditions

  • Independent. We report the effect of the product category. We do not compare providers by name, unless they choose to do so themselves in their own impact assessment.
  • No advice to bid without a condition. Bidding without a financing condition remains the buyer's choice and carries risks. A certificate provides cover only within the provider's terms. The research also examines whether buyers feel pressured to waive their financing condition.
  • Data minimisation. The seller and estate agent are not shown any of the buyer's income or asset data.
  • Consent. Source data is retrieved only with the customer's explicit consent, and a customer survey is conducted only with consent.
  • Aggregated. All results are reported in aggregated form, in line with our methodology.
