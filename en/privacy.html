For privacy questions, please contact [privacy / DPO e-mail address].

2. What data do we process through this website?

If you e-mail us, for example to request a quote, the whitepaper or the Hypotheekmonitor (Mortgage Monitor), we process the data you provide: your name, e-mail address, organisation, job title and the content of your message.

3. For what purpose and on what legal basis?

  • Responding to your request, such as sending the whitepaper or a quote. The legal basis is taking steps prior to entering into a contract or our legitimate interest.
  • Contact about our reports, only if you give your consent. You may withdraw that consent at any time.

4. How long do we retain your data?

No longer than necessary for the purpose for which you provided it. We retain contact details for a maximum of 2 years after the last contact, unless you become a client or a statutory retention obligation applies.

5. Data in our reports

Our reports contain only aggregated data that cannot be traced back to individual consumers or advisers. Segments with too few observations are not shown. We process the underlying data on the basis of agreements with the participating advisory firms. The advisory firm is the controller for the client file. The use for aggregated market research is set out in an agreement with each participating advisory firm. This agreement covers the purpose, pseudonymisation before analysis, retention periods and the prohibition on re-identification. The legal basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), supported by a balancing test and a DPIA. We do not use special categories of personal data, such as health data. See also our methodology.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your contact details. We share them only with service providers that support us, such as for hosting and e-mail, and only on the basis of a data processing agreement.

7. Cookies

This website does not place cookies and does not use any tracking or marketing tools.

8. Your rights

You may access, rectify or erase your data. You may also object to processing or request data portability. To do so, please contact us at [e-mail address]. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority).
