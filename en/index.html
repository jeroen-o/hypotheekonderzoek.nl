Product comparisons and ratings assess rates and terms on paper. We link the offering to what advisers actually do with it. You see which differences in price and terms lead to a different choice in practice.

Method & privacy

How the figures are produced

The main points are set out below. All definitions, calculation methods and limitations are described in our methodology.

Data source

The figures are based on mortgage and insurance applications registered via the intermediary channel. They reflect that channel and not the total Dutch mortgage market.

Calculation method

Market share is a weighted share: applications per provider divided by the total in the same period. For quarters and years it is therefore not the average of individual monthly percentages.

Aggregated only

We report only aggregated figures. Segments with too few observations are not shown, so that figures cannot be traced back to individual consumers.

Responsible lending and independent advice

Our insights are there to understand what happens in the market better. They are not intended to loosen acceptance criteria beyond what the Dutch Financial Supervision Act (Wft) and the Temporary Mortgage Credit Regulation allow. Insights into borrowing capacity and drop-out therefore always come with insight into the risks for the consumer. Clients have no influence on how the Blinqx advisory software works, such as the order or selection of providers.

Competition

The mortgage market is concentrated and transparent. We therefore apply fixed safeguards so that our insights do not restrict competition:

  • You see your own position against the market, never figures of individual competitors
  • Peer groups consist of at least five providers, with no single provider determining the outcome
  • Rate information comes only from publicly published rate sheets, with a time lag and as a range
  • No information on future or non-public prices, discounts or underwriting policy
  • Clients do not exchange information with one another through us.

Supervision and frameworks

Our methodology is aligned with publications by DNB, AFM, ECB, EBA, Kifid, NVB and the Verbond van Verzekeraars (Dutch Association of Insurers).
