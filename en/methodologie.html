1. Purpose and scope

Hypotheekonderzoek.nl™ provides market intelligence to mortgage lenders, ORV (mortgage term life) insurers, guarantee providers and advisory firms. The reports show the client's position relative to the market or to an anonymous peer group. They cover residential mortgages for private individuals and the insurance products advised alongside them.

2. Data source

  • Source: Blinqx mortgage advice software and Blinqx Connect CRM.
  • Units: calculations, applications and completions recorded by advisers in these systems.
  • Coverage: determined per study. Each report states the number of advisory firms, advisers, calculations, applications and completions in the period under review, together with the reference date.
  • Interest rate data: publicly available rate sheets from providers, converted into a single data model and recorded with source and date.
  • Additional open data: where indicated, publications from CBS (Statistics Netherlands), DNB and Kadaster (Netherlands Land Registry), among others.

The figures relate to the Blinqx network and the intermediary channel. They are not necessarily representative of the Dutch market as a whole, which also includes direct channels.

3. Definitions

TermDefinition
CalculationA run of a mortgage proposal that includes a provider. Multiple runs within one case file for the same provider in the same month count as one.
ApplicationA mortgage application sent from the case file to a provider
CompletionA mortgage for which the completion date (notarial transfer) has been recorded in the case file
Market shareThe number of applications to the provider divided by the total number of applications in the same period. This is a weighted share, not the average of individual monthly percentages.
ConversionThe number of applications divided by the number of calculations in which the provider appeared
Completion ratioThe number of completions divided by the number of applications in the same cohort
IndexThe share at a firm, in a region or in a segment, divided by the national share, multiplied by 100
First-time buyer / mover / remortgagerFirst-time buyer: first owner-occupied home. Mover: purchase combined with the sale of an owner-occupied home. Remortgager: new mortgage on the same home with a different provider.
LTVThe mortgage amount divided by the market value according to the valuation report. Without a valuation, we use the purchase price for existing properties and, for new-build properties, the purchase and construction price plus extras. Each report states what share of the LTV calculations is based on a valuation.
LTIThe mortgage amount divided by the household's qualifying income
Source validationA data item counts as source-validated if it was retrieved directly from an official source with the customer's consent, for example with the Procesversneller in eBlinqx. The retrieval date and the components are recorded per case file.
Validation rateThe share of the required data in a case file that has been source-validated, per component: income, pension, financial obligations and property ownership
Validation periodThe number of days between retrieval of the source data and the offer or application. Source data remain usable for 90 days.
Energy labelThe registered label of the collateral as recorded in the case file
Peer groupAn anonymous group of at least 5 comparable providers, in which no single party accounts for more than 25% of the volume

4. Calculation methods

Quartiles and ranges

Position within a peer group is expressed in quartiles. For interest rates, we show the lowest and highest rate, the 25th and 75th percentiles and the median, never the rate of an individual competitor.

Indicative reason for drop-off

For calculations that included a provider but did not lead to an application to that provider, we compare the interest rate, the maximum mortgage and the terms with those of the provider that was chosen. The largest difference is taken as the indicative reason. This is an inference, not a measured reason. The provider to which the application was sent is not reported.

Difference in borrowing capacity

The share of calculations in which the provider's maximum mortgage is more than 2% below the median of the providers modelled.

Forecast

The forecast of the client's own inflow is based on the historical relationship between calculations, applications and completions, with seasonal adjustment. The range shows the 80% prediction interval. The method is tested against actual outcomes every quarter (backtesting).

Indicative CO₂ emissions

Using the PCAF methodology, with an average emission per m² for each label class. Because actual consumption can vary widely within a label class, the result is indicative.

5. Data quality

  • Deduplication: duplicate calculations and applications within a single case file are merged.
  • Plausibility: values outside realistic limits are excluded, for example an LTV above 150% or a negative principal.
  • Missing values: reported separately as "unknown" and not imputed, unless explicitly stated.
  • Consistency: every month we reconcile calculations, applications and completions.
  • Revision: late registrations may still change earlier months. Figures become final 2 months after the end of the period. Until then they are marked as provisional.

6. Privacy

  • Reports contain aggregated data only, without personal data of consumers or advisers.
  • Segments with fewer than 25 observations are not shown.
  • The advisory firm is the data controller for the customer case file. The use of case file data for aggregated market research is set out in an agreement with each participating advisory firm. This agreement covers the purpose, pseudonymisation prior to analysis, retention periods and the prohibition on re-identification.
  • The legal basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), supported by a balancing test and a DPIA. We do not use special categories of personal data, such as health data.
  • Underlying data are processed in pseudonymised form and anonymised or deleted after 5 years.
  • Age, income and postcode are reported in bands only.

7. Competition law

  • A client sees only its own position relative to the market or an anonymous peer group.
  • No figures on individual competitors are provided.
  • Interest rate information comes exclusively from public rate sheets and is shown as a range, with a delay of at least 4 weeks.
  • No information is provided on future or non-public prices, discounts or underwriting policy.
  • Clients do not exchange information with one another via Hypotheekonderzoek.nl™.

8. Limitations

  • Coverage is limited to the Blinqx network. Direct channels and other advice software fall outside it.
  • Quality depends on what advisers record in the case file, for example the completion date and the energy label.
  • The reason for drop-off and the forecast are inferences subject to uncertainty.
  • Energy labels are an indication of energy efficiency, not a measurement of consumption.
