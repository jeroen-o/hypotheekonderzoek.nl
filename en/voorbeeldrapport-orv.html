Contents

    1 Executive summary

    Your position at a glance

    Five key findings

    1. Your share of term life applications rose from 15,8% to 18,1%, mainly among first-time buyers.
    2. Single applicants and seniors often see you in their quotes, but rarely choose you. Your premium there sits in the most expensive quartile.
    3. On mortgages from mortgage fund managers (regiepartijen) your share is twice as high as with the major banks.
    4. The sum insured averages 72% of the loan amount. For single applicants it is 52%, which points to a risk of underinsurance.
    5. From application to policy takes 9,4 working days with you, against 7,1 in the market. Advisers name underwriting speed as the main area for improvement.

    14 Methodology & competition law

    How the figures are compiled

    Source and definitions

    • Term life quotes, applications and policies recorded in the mortgage advice software and Blinqx Connect CRM
    • Market share is your term life applications divided by all term life applications in the same period
    • Premiums are taken from the premium quotes for standard profiles
    • See the full methodology

    Privacy and competition law

    • Aggregated figures only; segments with fewer than 25 observations are not shown
    • No health data or other special categories of personal data
    • Peer groups of at least 5 parties, without names
    • Premiums only as a range and after the fact
